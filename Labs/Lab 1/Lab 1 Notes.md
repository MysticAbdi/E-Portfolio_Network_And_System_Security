# Lab 1 - CIA Triad and Incident Analysis - Lab Notes

## Learning goals

- Recall the three core security properties: confidentiality, integrity, availability.
- Practice applying CIA thinking to real incidents instead of only learning the terms.
- Start building a habit of explaining security issues in plain language.

## Activities

1. Reviewed definitions of the CIA triad:
   - Confidentiality: keeping information secret from unauthorised people.
   - Integrity: keeping information accurate and preventing unauthorised changes.
   - Availability: keeping systems and data accessible to authorised users when needed.

2. Looked at a set of real world incidents such as:
   - Large scale personal data breaches.
   - Ransomware attacks that shut down services.
   - Supply chain compromises.

3. For each incident I asked:
   - Which CIA property was hit the hardest.
   - Which secondary properties were also affected.
   - What technical or organisational failures made the incident possible.

4. Summarised each incident in a few lines, highlighting:
   - What happened.
   - The main CIA impact.
   - One or two lessons that could prevent a repeat.

## Key points noted

- Many incidents affect more than one CIA property at once.
- The same weakness, such as poor patching or weak authentication, can cause different types of impact depending on the system.
- Thinking in CIA terms helps structure reports and conversations with non technical people.
