# Lab 7 - Penetration Testing Basics - Reflection

This week joined together many of the earlier skills into something closer to a real penetration testing workflow.
Even though the tools I wrote were small, they helped me understand what larger tools like Nmap and commercial scanners are doing behind the scenes.

The biggest takeaway is that technical ability must be matched with responsibility.
Running a port scan or header check is not inherently good or bad, it depends completely on consent and scope.
The labs made that clear by focusing on test environments and by repeatedly stressing permission.

In the future, if I take part in security testing, I will be more mindful of documenting scope, keeping logs of what I run, and treating all targets as if they belong to someone who has trusted me with access.
